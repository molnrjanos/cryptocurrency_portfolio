# Cryptocurrency Portfolio Dashboard
### Financial Analysis | Data Visualization | Microsoft Excel

## About the Project

I created this dashboard to bring different aspects of cryptocurrency portfolio management together in one place. My goal was to make financial and market information easier to follow, understand, and compare through a clear and visually engaging layout.

I wanted to combine the analytical side of finance with the creative side of dashboard design, making sure that the information is not only useful but also easy to navigate.

## What You Can Find Inside

The dashboard brings together several areas of cryptocurrency and portfolio monitoring:

- **Portfolio Allocation:** An overview of weighted cryptocurrency pools, including Solana, Arbitrum, and Ethereum.
- **Market Sentiment:** A Fear & Greed Index to provide insight into overall market sentiment.
- **Cash Flow:** Monthly cash flow tracking and visualization.
- **Market Activity:** Volatility and trading volume comparisons.
- **Financial Overview:** Commission tracking, withdrawal prices, and profit withdrawals.
- **Portfolio Insights:** Visual representations of pool distribution, cryptocurrency performance, and crowdfunding information.

## Design & Approach

I focused on creating a clean, dark-themed dashboard where different types of financial information could work together without making the overall layout feel overwhelming.

I used different chart types, contrasting colors, and a structured layout to make the data easier to read and explore.

I also organized the workbook into separate sections for raw data, processing, controls, and the dashboard itself, keeping the underlying calculations and the visual presentation structured.

## What I Wanted to Achieve

For me, this project was about more than putting charts together. I wanted to explore how financial data can be presented in a way that makes sense to the person using it.

I enjoy working at the intersection of numbers, technology, and design, and this project gave me an opportunity to bring those interests together.

I believe good financial reporting should not only provide information but also help people understand what they are looking at and make better-informed decisions.
